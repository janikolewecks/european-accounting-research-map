About this data. Every submission is classified by large language models on a fixed vocabulary of 25 topics, 14 method families and 8 data source categories, using identical prompts and settings in every congress year. Topics and methods are multi-label, so shares within a dimension do not sum to 100. Data sources are single-label. Submissions appearing twice under an identical title by the same author are counted once. The file behind this page contains no author names, titles, abstracts, affiliations or institution-level information. Figures may differ from the published paper where that paper applies additional sample restrictions.
